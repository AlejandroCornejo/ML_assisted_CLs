                                                           computing environments. Our workflow is validated through a case study focusing on the thermal dynamics of a
                                                           motor, a multiphysics problem involving convective heat transfer and mechanical components. The projection-
                                                           based reduced-order model is designed to deliver a real-time prognosis tool that could enable rapid and safe motor

                                                           for scalable, real-time digital twin applications in computational mechanics across multiple industries.

      are projected (hence the qualifier “projection-based”). Moreover,
      we accomplish the decoupling of the ROMs from full-dimensional
      variables through a hyperreduction mesh sampling and weighting
