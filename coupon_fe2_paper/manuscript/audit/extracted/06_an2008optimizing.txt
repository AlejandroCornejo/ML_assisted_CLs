Figure 1: Overview of Cubature Optimization: Given a subspace deformation model with N elements, we generate (sample or simulate) a
set of training poses for input to the cubature optimization preprocess. The optimization procedure estimates nN cubature elements/points,
and associated nonnegative weights, wi , such that the the cubature approximation of f(q) well-approximates the training force/pose data.
At runtime, the cubature scheme uses the force response of only the n cubature elements to provide a fast approximation to internal forces,
therein accelerating integration of subspace deformation dynamics.
