Abstract We present a sufficient condition under which a weak solution of the Euler-
Lagrange equations in nonlinear elasticity is already a global minimizer of the corresponding
                                                                              (F T F ) = W
elastic energy functional. This criterion is applicable to energies W (F ) = W             (C)
which are convex with respect to the right Cauchy-Green tensor C = F T F , where F denotes
the gradient of deformation. Examples of such energies exhibiting a blow up for det F → 0
are given.

Keywords Nonlinear elasticity · Convexity · Global minimizers · Second Piola-Kirchhoff
stress tensor
